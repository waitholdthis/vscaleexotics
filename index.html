Provenance

Seventeen years of
unbroken records

Every animal that leaves this facility carries a lineage document going back to the founder pair. Not a certificate we printed the morning it shipped — a record we have been keeping since 2009, with weights, feed responses, shed cycles, clutch data and photographs of both parents.

It matters most where it is hardest to verify. Locality green tree pythons are routinely sold as pure Sorong or pure Biak when they are neither. Axanthic ball pythons are sold without naming the line, which makes them worthless to a breeder. Dwarf percentages in reticulated pythons are quoted from memory. We publish ours and we will show you the working.

How the facility runs

Our position

What we will not sell you

We do not breed, buy or place any animal from the Spider / Champagne / Hidden Gene Woma locus. Every animal carrying those genes has a neurological syndrome, and no homozygous form has ever been produced alive. The Gene Lab will still calculate them, because refusing to show you the maths helps nobody — but you will not find one in our collection.

We do not place Jaguar carpet pythons. We do not power-feed to hit a sale weight. We do not ship into a marginal forecast to meet a date. And we will decline a sale outright if the enclosure you describe is not one the animal can live well in.

Gene Lab

A calculator that
knows what a locus is

Pair a Mojave with a Lesser in most public morph calculators and you will be told to expect a “Mojave Lesser”. There is no such animal. They are alleles of the same locus, and what you actually get is a Blue-Eyed Leucistic.

Ours models loci rather than genes, so allelic complexes resolve correctly, named combinations appear where they should, and non-viable genotypes are removed from the live clutch and reported separately instead of quietly inflating everything else.

It also separates what the animal is from what you can see — which is where “66% possible het” actually comes from, and why it is a conditional probability rather than a fixed number.
